What we do on the block

Silviculture is the whole job here. Our crews cover the full rotation, from planting bare land to a sprayed, pruned and thinned stand that is worth harvesting.

Establishment

Planting

Seedlings in the ground at the right spacing, in the right season. Stocking rates hit the target, not just near it.

Tending

Pruning

Clean lift pruning, cut flush and tidy. Clearwood is where the money sits at harvest, so every pass counts.

Tending

Thinning by chainsaw

Final crop stems picked, the rest felled. Your stand ends up at the stocking your forestry plan calls for.

Establishment

Spot spraying

Weeds knocked back around young trees so the crop gets the light and water. Targeted spraying, not blanket coverage.

Tending

Chemical thinning

Unwanted stems treated and left standing to die off. A safer, faster option on steep or hard-to-work country.

Tending

Regen pulling

Wilding and regeneration seedlings pulled out before they crowd the crop or spread across the block.

A Hawke's Bay crew since 2018

FM Silvi Limited is a Safetree certified forestry company, established in 2018 and based in Hawke's Bay. Our office is at 675 Wakarara Road, RD2, Ongaonga.

We work along the Napier–Taupō and Napier–Wairoa highways, and in Masterton, Taupō and Rotorua. Our mixed crew is made up of locals and men from Fiji and Vanuatu, all trained in first aid and in fighting forest fires.

  • Safetree certified, established 2018
  • First aid and forest firefighting trained
  • Hawke's Bay, Taupō, Rotorua and Masterton

Common questions

The things forest owners and managers usually ask before booking a crew.

What areas do you work in?

We're based in Ongaonga, Central Hawke's Bay, and work along the Napier–Taupō and Napier–Wairoa highways, as well as in Masterton, Taupō and Rotorua. If your block is somewhere else, give us a call and we'll tell you straight whether we can get a crew there.

What work do you do?

Planting, spot spraying, pruning, chemical thinning, thinning by chainsaw and regen pulling. Silviculture is all we do, from getting seedlings in the ground to a pruned, thinned stand.

Are you Safetree certified?

Yes. FM Silvi Limited is Safetree certified, current from 7 December 2025 to 7 December 2027. We also received the 2019 Health and Safety Silviculture Excellence Award for the Hawke's Bay region. You can see both in the Awards & certification section.

How are your crews trained?

Our crew is trained in first aid and in fighting forest fires. Ratu, our foreman, runs tailgate meetings and organises the work plans on every job.

How do I get a quote?

Call us on 027 341 0107 or email with the rough numbers: how many hectares, the age of the stand and where it is. We'll come back to you with a price and when we can start.

Where is your office?

675 Wakarara Road, RD2, Ongaonga. Most of our time is spent out on the block, so call ahead before dropping in.

Talk to us about your block

Ring or email with rough numbers: hectares, age of the stand and where it is. We will come back with a price and a window.
