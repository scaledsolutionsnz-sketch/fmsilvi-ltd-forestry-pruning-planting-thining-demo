What we do on the block

Silviculture is the whole job here. One crew, the full rotation: from bare land to a pruned, thinned stand that is worth harvesting.

Establishment

Tree planting

Radiata and natives in the ground at the right spacing, in the right season. Stocking rates hit the target, not just near it.

Tending

Pruning

Clean lift pruning, cut flush and tidy. Clearwood is where the money sits at harvest, so every pass counts.

Tending

Thinning to waste

Final crop stems picked, the rest dropped. Your stand ends up at the stocking your forestry plan calls for.

Farm forestry

Woodlots & shelterbelts

Small farm woodlots and shelterbelts pruned and kept in shape. The jobs bigger outfits will not drive out for.

Establishment

New forest blocks

Turning a paddock or retired hill face into a forest. We work to your plan from land prep through to first tending.

Contracting

Crew contracting

Crews for forest managers who need planting or tending covered across bigger estates. Priced by the hectare or the day.

A local crew, not a call centre

fmsilvi ltd works out of Ongaonga, doing silviculture for forest owners across Central Hawke's Bay and beyond. Planting, pruning and thinning is all we do, and we do it in most weather.

You deal with the people doing the work. Ring, describe the block, and you will get a straight answer on whether we can take it on and what it will cost.
